# Code of Conduct

## Commitment
OVX contributors are expected to maintain a professional, respectful, constructive environment.

## Expected behavior
- be respectful;
- debate technical ideas without personal attacks;
- welcome questions and differing experience levels;
- provide actionable feedback;
- respect privacy;
- avoid harassment or discriminatory conduct.

## Unacceptable behavior
- harassment or threats;
- discriminatory language;
- personal attacks;
- publishing private information without permission;
- deliberate disruption.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions or participation that violates these expectations. Publish a dedicated conduct contact before the community expands.
